Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common questions about our phones, warranty, deliveries and repairs

📲 Buying and deliveries country wide

Are all phones brand new and original?

Yes! All phones sold on our website are 100% genuine, brand new, and come in factory-sealed boxes with standard manufacturer warranties.

How long does delivering take?

Standard delivery takes 1 to 2 business days. But that entirely depends on where you are . It could sometimes take hours

Can I trade in my old phone for a new one?

Yes, we offer trade-ins! Bring your old phone to our local store or select the "Trade-In" option during online checkout to receive instant credit toward your purchase.

🛠️ Warranty & Service

What is covered under the store warranty?

Our standard 6-month warranty covers manufacturer defects, battery malfunctions, and motherboard issues. Physical or liquid damage is not covered unless extended coverage is purchased.

Cracked screens, or defected screens that have been caused by the customer are also not coverd

How do I submit a device for screen repair?

You can visit our flagship repair store or request a prepaid delivery box through our Contact Us page to mail your device directly to our technicians.

💳 Payments & Returns

What is your return policy?

We offer a 1-day return or exchange policy for all unopened or defective items. Please keep your original purchase receipt.

What payment options do you accept?

We accept Visa, MasterCard, Momo pay, Apple Pay, and local mobile payments.

Still have questions?

If you couldn't find the answer to your question, feel free to reach out to our team.
